## Creating a timer

Click a mode button on the left of the toolbar to create a timer of that type. Four modes are available:

- **Countdown**: set a target time and count down to zero;
- **Timer**: counts up from zero to track how long things take;
- **Stopwatch**: millisecond precision for sports and cooking;
- **Alarm**: rings at a set time.

All four modes can run side by side in one window, each timing and alerting on its own.

## Start, pause and resume

Every timer has its own controls:

- Click **Start / Pause / Resume** on a timer to control its running state;
- Click **Reset** to return the timer to its initial state;
- The time display updates in real time while a timer runs.

## Recurring timing

Countdowns support **cycle timing**: with the cycle timer toggle on, a countdown restarts automatically when it reaches zero — perfect for workout intervals, cooking rotations and other repeating cycles.

## Alert options

Countdowns and Alarms support several alert options, controlled by the **notification toggle** and **ring setting**:

- **Sound alerts**: a ringtone plays when time is up; choose it in the ring setting;
- **Notification alerts**: a pop-up notification appears when time is up;
- **System notifications**: alarms still ring through the system even if the app is closed, so no important moment slips by.

## Tips

- Timer names and colors are customizable, so you can tell timers apart at a glance;
- All timers can be pinned on top of your screen — see [On-top Pinning Help](topmost-reminder-pin/);
