## What on-top pinning is

The signature feature of Topmost Reminder: all timers can be **pinned on top of your screen**. They stay visible above other windows without interfering with your work in other apps. For critical timing tasks, you will not miss alerts even when you are focused.

## Pin and unpin

- Click the **pin button** on a timer's toolbar to pin that timer on top of your screen;
- Click the pin button again to unpin; the timer returns to the app window.

## Repositioning

Pinned timers can be dragged to any position on your screen:

- Press **Ctrl+F3** to enter edit mode (or click the edit button on the toolbar);
- Drag the timer to the target position with the mouse;
- Click the confirm button on the left, or press **Ctrl+F3** again to stop editing.

## Tips

- Pinned timers never block mouse interaction with other software;
- Multiple timers can be pinned at once, each placed in a different corner of your screen;
- When a countdown or alarm is up, you get a sound, notification or system alert even if the app is not in the foreground.
