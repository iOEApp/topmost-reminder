## Scope

This policy covers **Topmost Reminder** (置顶计时器 / 置頂計時器), a desktop timer
utility published by OEApp for Windows 10/11.

## What Topmost Reminder collects

Topmost Reminder is designed to run entirely on your device.

- **No account is required.** You can use Topmost Reminder without registering or signing in.
- **No personal data is uploaded.** Timers, groups and related settings are stored locally
  on your computer. Topmost Reminder does not transmit them to us.
- **No screen content is uploaded.** The app only displays timer overlays on top of other
  apps on your own machine; it does not capture, record or send your screen to any server.

## Purchases

The free download is distributed through the Microsoft Store. The optional Full version
in-app purchase is processed by the Microsoft Store. We receive only the aggregated sales
reporting the store provides to developers; we never receive your payment card details.
